# 2026-10-05

## getDateOffset learns a year unit

### The request

Give the Personal vault a yearly note, built the way the daily note is built: one
body with `{{key}}` placeholders, rendered identically by obsidian.nvim and by
Templater.

### What was missing

`getDateOffset` handled `week`, `month` and `quarter`. A yearly note needs the
previous and next year for its navigation bar, and no substitution produced them.
The 16 keys the plugin exposed had no year arithmetic at all.

The new branch sets month 6, day 15 and noon before adding the offset. Mid-June,
mid-month and midday means neither month length nor a DST transition can push the
result into an adjacent year. The quarter branch already used day 15 and noon for
the same reason.

`YEAR_PREVIOUS_YEAR` and `YEAR_NEXT_YEAR` call it. The week, month and quarter
units are untouched, and were re-checked after the change: `2026-W40 / 2026-W42`,
`2026-09 / 2026-11`, `2026-Q3 / 2027-Q1`.

### A trap worth recording

Editing a vault's `.lazy.lua` invalidates nvim's exrc trust. nvim then refuses to
load it, `:Obsidian template` cannot resolve any template, and every
Makefile-driven note creation in that vault fails. It is silent: nvim still
writes the file, with the plugin's frontmatter and no body.

Run `:trust` on `.lazy.lua` after editing it. The vault's Makefile now deletes a
body-less note and exits non-zero rather than leaving one behind.
